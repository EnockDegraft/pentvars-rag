# Admission Entry Requirements — Pentecost University

## WASSCE / SSSCE Requirements (Standard Entry)
Applicants need credit passes in three (3) core subjects, including English Language, Mathematics, and Integrated Science or Social Studies, plus three (3) relevant elective subjects.

- Minimum aggregate: not exceeding 24 for SSSCE/GBCE, or 36 for WASSCE.
- Acceptable "credit pass" grades: WASSCE grades A1–C6, or SSSCE/GBCE grades 1–6.
- Grades that do NOT qualify: SSSCE/GBCE grades E–F, or WASSCE grades D7–F9.
- Successful applicants are admitted at Level 100 (first year).

## Mature Entrant Requirements
Applicants who do not have the standard qualifications above may still qualify as mature entrants if they meet the following:
- Must be at least 25 years old at the time of application.
- Must pass a competitive entrance examination consisting of an English paper (essay, comprehension, and grammar) and an aptitude assessment.
- Must achieve a minimum grade of D (50%) on each paper.
- Must successfully complete an admissions interview.
- Successful mature entrants are admitted at Level 100.

## International Student Requirements
International applicants may apply using any of the following qualifications:
- International Baccalaureate (IB)
- IGCSE / GCSE
- American Grade 12–13 examinations
- Any qualification equivalent to SSSCE / WASSCE / GBCE (subject to GTEC evaluation for equivalency)

Additional requirements for international students:
- Must pass an interview and an English proficiency test.
- Must provide a valid passport and travel documents.
- Must provide police clearance from their home country.
- Must register with the Ghana Police Service.
- Must provide verified immigration/visa documentation.
