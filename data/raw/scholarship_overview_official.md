# Scholarships and Financial Aid (Official Summary) — Pentecost University

## Awards available
1. **Full Scholarship** — covers **100% of tuition fees**.
2. **Half Scholarship** — covers **50% of tuition fees**.
3. **Student Bursaries** — a monetary award of an amount determined by the
   Scholarship Committee.
4. **Church Member Discount** — a **10% discount on tuition** for all
   members of The Church of Pentecost.

## Eligibility
- Must be an admitted or enrolled student.
- **New students:** a WASSCE aggregate of **6–18**.
- **Continuing students:** a minimum **CGPA of 3.00**.
- Evidence of creative and innovative skills (proof of innovation and
  creativity), and/or exceptional commitment to ethical leadership backed
  by national/international awards.

## Required documents
1. Unconditional admission letter
2. Result slip or transcript
3. Testimonial from a former school or mentor
4. Recommendation letter from a teacher or counsellor
5. An essay on why you should be awarded the scholarship — **250 words
   maximum**
6. Two passport photographs taken within the last 6 months
7. Birth or baptismal certificate, or a sworn affidavit
8. A self-addressed envelope
9. A parent/guardian pay slip or payroll record
10. A death certificate or a witness statement from a religious leader
    (for hardship cases)
11. A pension letter, if applicable
12. A copy of an ID (school, NHIS, voter, or national ID)

All documents must be certified (stamped and signed) by recognised
officials.

## How and when to apply
- Apply **after receiving admission**, before the academic year begins —
  the window runs **March to July**.
- Seal all forms in an envelope and submit to the **Chairman of the
  Scholarship Committee**.
- Applications are reviewed at the start of the academic year; shortlisted
  candidates are interviewed; decisions are announced after the interviews.

## Tenure and renewal
- Maximum tenure **4 years**, subject to the student's continued
  eligibility and the availability of funds.
- The student must maintain a **CGPA of at least 3.0** and full-time
  enrolment, follow the approved programme, and not change programmes
  during the award.
- A bursary is a **one-time** award.

## Withdrawal
The award is withdrawn if the student's CGPA falls below 3.0, the student
receives a disciplinary sanction, or the student defers their programme.
