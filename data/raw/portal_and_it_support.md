# Student Portal and IT Support — Pentecost University

This page covers the common technology problems students report to the ICT
Directorate and how to resolve them.

## Student e-portal login
The e-portal is at https://eportal.pentvars.edu.gh. Your username is
normally your student index number. Your first-time password is the one
issued with your admission letter.

- **Forgotten password**: use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
  A reset link is sent to the email address held on your record. If no
  email arrives within a few minutes, check the spam folder, then contact
  ICT.
- **"Invalid credentials" but the password is correct**: you may be
  entering the index number in the wrong format (no spaces, correct
  prefix). After five failed attempts the account locks for 30 minutes.
- **Account locked or password reset email goes to an old address**:
  email ict@pentvars.edu.gh from any address, or visit the ICT Directorate
  in person with your student ID so they can verify you and update the
  recovery email.
- **Page loads but rejects login right after fees payment**: the portal can
  take a few hours to sync a new payment. If it is still blocking you the
  next day, send ICT your name, index number and payment reference.

## Student email
Students are given an official email account for all university
correspondence, including scholarship and results notifications.

- First sign-in uses the credentials from ICT during orientation.
- Reset requests go to ict@pentvars.edu.gh.
- Always check this account (and its spam folder) for scholarship decisions
  and results — notices are not sent to personal email.

## Campus wifi
- Connect to the campus network and sign in with your e-portal username and
  password.
- If authentication fails, confirm your e-portal login works first — wifi
  uses the same account.
- Weak signal in a specific building should be reported to ICT with the
  building and room so it can be logged.

## Learning management system (LMS / Moodle)
- The LMS uses the same username and password as the e-portal.
- If a course you are registered for is not showing, it can take 24–48
  hours after registration to appear. If it is still missing, confirm on
  the e-portal that the registration went through, then contact ICT with a
  screenshot.
- Upload problems are almost always a file-size limit or an unsupported
  file type — export to PDF and try again before contacting support.

## Payment portal not loading
- The payment portal is https://interpayafrica.com/puc/student.
- If it will not load, try a different browser or network before assuming
  your account is the problem.
- A payment that debited your bank but does not show on the e-portal is a
  Finance Office matter, not ICT — see the Fees and Payments page.

## Contacting ICT
- Email: ict@pentvars.edu.gh
- Phone: +233 30 241 7057 / 7058
- Digital Services Unit support lines: +233 24 490 6386 / +233 24 597 0073,
  and a 24-hour WhatsApp-only support chatline (find the current chat link
  on the e-portal/admission portal login help page).
- In person: ICT Directorate, main campus, during working hours. Bring your
  student ID.
- Include your name, index number, a description of the problem, and a
  screenshot of any error message.

## Reporting other campus problems
ICT is only for portal/LMS/wifi/email issues. For anything else, the right
first stop depends on what's wrong:
- **Hall/hostel issue** (plumbing, electricity, safety, roommate conflict):
  the Hall Warden or Senior Resident first; unresolved matters go to the
  Dean of Students.
- **General welfare, safety, or a dispute with another student**: the
  Office of the Dean of Student Affairs (ODSA).
- **Library issue** (catalogue, access, a book problem): library@pentvars.edu.gh.
- **Suspected criminal conduct or a security concern**: campus security —
  students must be ready to show their ID card to security on request at
  any time; serious matters are also reportable to the Ghana Police Service.
- Not sure who owns it? Start with the Dean of Students' office or the main
  reception line (+233 30 241 7057 / 8) and ask to be redirected — that's
  the University's general front door for anything that doesn't have an
  obvious department.
