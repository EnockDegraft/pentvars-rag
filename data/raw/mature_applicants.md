# Mature Applicants and the Mature Entrance Examination — Pentecost University

The Mature route lets people without the standard WASSCE/SSSCE grades enter
at **Level 100**.

## Who qualifies
- **At least 25 years old** at the time of application, with a birth
  certificate that is at least one year old.
- **At least 2 years of working experience**, with or without formal
  education.

## The Mature Entrance Examination (MEE)
Four (4) papers:
- **English** — Essay; Comprehension; Grammar and Usage.
- **General Paper(s)** — Quantitative Methods; Critical Thinking; Current
  Affairs.

**Pass mark:** you must pass **all four papers**, scoring at least **half
(50%) of the total marks on each paper**, to be invited to the interview.
Final admission depends on passing the interview.

## Preparation classes
Preparation classes run on **Saturdays for four (4) consecutive weeks**
before the examination, from **7:00 am to 4:00 pm**, with 30-minute breaks
between sessions.

## How to apply
Apply through the Application Forms page
(https://pentvars.edu.gh/admission-forms/). The Mature Entrance
Examinations Coordinator can be reached on +233 30 241 7057 / 8 or
+233 249435191. Examination fees are not published online — confirm with
the coordinator. Admissions for the Mature route are handled through the
College of Foundation and Professional Studies (COFOPS).
