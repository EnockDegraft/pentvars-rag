# How to Apply — Application Procedure — Pentecost University

## Online application (recommended)
1. Go to the Pentecost University homepage.
2. Click **Application Forms**, then **Online Application**
   (https://eportal.pentvars.edu.gh/pueportalonline/users1.php).
3. **Register for a login PIN.**
4. Fill in the forms carefully.
5. Upload certified copies of your certificates and result slip (and any
   other relevant documents).
6. Upload a scanned passport-size photograph.
7. Click **Send** to submit.

## Postal / in-person application
1. Download the application form from the website.
2. Complete it in full.
3. Attach certified copies of your certificates, result slip and any other
   relevant documents, plus **3 passport pictures, one endorsed**.
4. Send to: **The Academic Registrar, Pentecost University, P.O. Box KN
   1739, Kaneshie-Accra**, or deliver it in person to the **Academic
   Affairs Office, Sowutuom Campus**.

## Documents to prepare
- Certified copies of certificates and result slip(s).
- Passport-size photograph(s) — one endorsed for postal applications.
- For postgraduate study: 2 reference letters, an original transcript and a
  certified degree copy (see the Postgraduate Programmes page; the graduate
  application form costs GH¢120, or US$120 for international applicants).

## Decisions and timeline
- Admission letters are sent to applicants' **email addresses**.
- International applications may begin up to a year before the intended
  start date; successful international students receive their admission
  letter by email **within one month** of applying.
- Undergraduate application fees, exact deadlines and the decision timeline
  for local applicants are not published on the procedure page — confirm
  with the Admissions Office.
