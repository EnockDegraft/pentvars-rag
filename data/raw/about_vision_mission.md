# About Pentecost University

## Vision
Pentecost University aspires to be a distinguished Christian institution, recognized for its excellence in teaching, research, and community impact.

## Mission
Pentecost University is committed to developing resourceful, value-driven graduates while generating and sharing knowledge through research and innovation in collaboration with industry, commerce, and the public sector.

## Core Values
1. **Godliness** — Maintaining a reverent institutional culture centered on Christian principles in all endeavors.
2. **Integrity** — Demonstrating honesty, accountability, and ethical conduct across all organizational activities.
3. **Excellence** — Pursuing high standards in pedagogical approaches, scholarly inquiry, and community engagement.
4. **Agility** — Exhibiting responsiveness to evolving circumstances at regional and worldwide scales with persistent commitment.
5. **Inclusiveness** — Promoting equitable access and leveraging organizational diversity to strengthen institutional outcomes.

## Accreditation and Affiliation
Pentecost University is accredited by the National Accreditation Board (NAB), Ghana, and is affiliated to the University of Ghana, Legon. It is also affiliated with The Church of Pentecost.

## Location
Pentecost University is located at Kaneshie, Accra, Ghana (P.O. Box KN 1739).

## Academic Structure
The university is organized into the following faculties and schools:
- School of Theology, Mission & Leadership
- Faculty of Engineering, Science and Computing
- Faculty of Business Administration
- Faculty of Health and Allied Sciences
- Faculty of Law
- Faculty of Education
- College of Foundation and Professional Studies

The university also operates a Graduate School offering postgraduate programmes, along with professional development partnerships and research centers.
