# Fees and Payment Problems — Pentecost University

This page covers what to do when something goes wrong with fee payment.
General fee structure and payment methods are on the Tuition Fees page.

## Payment made but not showing on the e-portal
1. Wait a few hours — bank transfers and the payment portal can take time
   to reconcile, and manual bank payments are posted on the next working
   day.
2. If it still does not show the next working day, take proof of payment to
   the Finance Office (Cash Office): the bank slip or transaction
   reference, the amount, the date, and the account it was paid into
   (Pentecost University, Prudential Bank, Tesano Branch).
3. The Cash Office posts the payment to your account manually and reissues
   your registration invoice.

Keep every payment receipt until you have confirmed the balance on the
e-portal — you will need the reference number to trace a missing payment.

## Cannot register because of an outstanding balance
- Online registration requires at least 70% of tuition plus all other
  required fees (academic user fee, SRC dues, Pentvars Journal fee,
  Technology User fee).
- If you have paid enough but the portal still blocks registration, it is
  usually an unreconciled payment — follow the steps above.
- If you genuinely cannot raise 70% by the deadline, speak to the Finance
  Office about a short payment arrangement before the deadline, not after.

## Payment arrangement / instalments
- The standard schedule is already split: at least 70% before online
  registration, the balance before revision week.
- A student who cannot meet this can request a written instalment
  arrangement from the Finance Office. Bring evidence of your situation.
  An approved arrangement lets you register; missing an agreed instalment
  can void it and make you ineligible to sit examinations.

## Receipts and statements
- An official receipt is available on the e-portal after each posted
  payment.
- For a stamped hard-copy receipt or a full statement of your fee account,
  request it at the Cash Office.

## Refunds and overpayments
- Overpaid amounts are normally held as a credit against your next
  semester's fees.
- A cash refund (for example after withdrawing from the programme) is
  requested in writing to the Finance Office and is subject to any
  non-refundable charges stated in your admission terms.
- Refunds are paid to the student's or sponsor's bank account, not in cash,
  and take a few weeks to process.

## Late registration charges
- Registering after the published deadline attracts a late fee.
- If the delay was caused by a university-side error (for example an
  unreconciled payment you had reported in time), ask the Finance Office in
  writing to waive the late charge and attach your evidence.
