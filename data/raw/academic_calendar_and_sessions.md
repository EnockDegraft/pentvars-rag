# Academic Calendar and Study Sessions — Pentecost University

## Study sessions
Pentecost University runs its programmes in different **sessions/schools**:
- **Regular School** — weekday daytime study, for September intakes and
  continuing students.
- **Weekend School** — for students who study on weekends (with a Friday
  evening slot for some activities such as mentorship).
- **Evening School** — evening classes for working students.
- Graduate programmes run on a mix of weekend, evening and full-time modes
  and use a **trimester** structure in some cases (three trimesters per
  year), while undergraduate programmes use two **semesters** per year.

Intakes: the main intake is in **September**; there is a second intake in
**January**.

## Academic calendar
The full academic calendar — with registration windows, add/drop
deadlines, lecture periods, revision weeks, examination periods, breaks,
matriculation and congregation dates — is published each year as a
**downloadable document** on the Academic Calendar page, separately for:
- 2025/2026 (September intakes and all continuing students, Regular School)
- January 2027 intakes
- January 2027 intakes and all continuing students (Weekend School)

Because exact dates change each year and differ by session, always check
the current downloadable calendar on
https://pentvars.edu.gh/academics/academic-calender/ or the noticeboards
rather than relying on last year's dates.

## Typical rhythm each semester/trimester
Registration opens → registration deadline → add/drop period → teaching
weeks → revision week → examinations → results (provisional, then
confirmed after Academic Board approval) → break. Resit/supplementary
examinations for failed papers are scheduled before the next academic
period and are registered and paid for separately.
