# Student Life and Support Services — Pentecost University

Pentecost University provides a range of support services to students beyond academics:

- **Housing** — On-campus and affiliated lodging facilities are available to students.
- **Counselling Unit** — Provides mental health and personal counselling support.
- **Chaplaincy Services** — Provides spiritual care in line with the university's Christian identity.
- **Mentorship Programmes** — Peer support and mentorship programmes for students.
- **Clubs and Societies** — A range of recreational clubs, societies, and sports/entertainment programmes.
- **Students' Representative Council (SRC)** — The recognised student leadership and representative body.
- **Library and E-Learning** — Access to the university library and online e-learning platforms to support academic work.
