# About Pentecost University — History, Charter and Leadership

## Founding and history
Pentecost University was founded by **The Church of Pentecost (COP)**. It grew
out of **Pentecost Bible College**, which originally trained lay leaders and
full-time ministers for the Church.

- The university (then **Pentecost University College**, PUC) was officially
  inaugurated on **22 May 2003** by then President of Ghana, J. A. Kufuor,
  at the Sowutuom campus.
- The first PUC Council was inducted on **6 May 2004**.
- It received accreditation from Ghana's **National Accreditation Board (NAB)**
  in **November 2004**.
- It was awarded its **Presidential Charter on 28 May 2020** by President
  Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo, becoming a fully chartered university and
  changing its name from *Pentecost University College* to
  **Pentecost University**.
- Before the charter, the college's programmes were run in affiliation with
  the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), the
  University of Cape Coast (UCC) and the University of Ghana (UG).

## Motto, vision and mission
- **Motto:** "Transformation and Service".
- **Vision:** "Pentecost University aspires to be a distinguished Christian
  institution, recognized for its excellence in teaching, research, and
  community impact."
- **Mission:** "Pentecost University is committed to developing resourceful,
  value-driven graduates while generating and sharing knowledge through
  research and innovation in collaboration with industry, commerce, and the
  public sector."

## Core values
1. **Godliness** — uphold a God-fearing culture and maintain
   Christ-centredness in all actions.
2. **Integrity** — demonstrate transparency, honesty, accountability and
   integrity in all actions.
3. **Excellence** — exhibit excellence in teaching, learning, research and
   service to humanity.
4. **Agility** — respond swiftly and effectively to shifting national and
   global trends.
5. **Inclusiveness** — foster equal opportunities for all and leverage
   diverse perspectives.

## Location and size
- Main campus: **Sowutuom**, in the western part of Accra, Greater Accra
  Region, Ghana. Postal address: P.O. Box KN 1739, Kaneshie, Accra. Digital
  (Ghana Post GPS) address: **GC-003-9632**.
- Student population: approximately **3,000**.

## Academic structure
- Faculties/Schools: Faculty of Engineering, Science and Computing (FESAC);
  Faculty of Business Administration (FBA); Faculty of Health and Allied
  Sciences (FHAS); School of Theology, Mission & Leadership (STML); Faculty
  of Law.
- **Pentecost University Graduate School (PUGS)** for postgraduate study.
- **College of Foundation and Professional Studies (COFOPS)** for access,
  professional and short-course programmes.

## Leadership and governance
- **Chancellor:** Apostle Eric Nyamekye (Chairman of The Church of Pentecost).
- **Chairperson of Council:** Elizabeth Ankumah.
- **Vice-Chancellor:** Rev. Prof. Kwabena Agyapong-Kodua (appointed 1 June
  2020) — an academic, researcher and ordained minister with a background in
  Systems Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Leadership and Theology, and
  previous posts at the UK universities of Warwick, Nottingham and
  Loughborough. Named "Most Outstanding Personality in Private University
  Education" in 2025.
- Under recent leadership the university has been recognised as Overall Best
  Private University in Ghana and Most Innovative Private University.

## International partnerships
