# College of Foundation and Professional Studies (COFOPS) — Pentecost University

COFOPS offers access pathways, professional certifications and short
courses for people seeking foundational learning, a professional
certificate, or career advancement. Admissions run in intakes through the
year ("admissions are in progress").

## Access and foundation
- Mature Entrance Examination (see the Mature Applicants page)
- Adult Education

## Language and communication
- Language proficiency courses in English, French, Spanish and Chinese

## Business, IT and professional certifications
- Association of Business Executives (ABE) — with a 1-year top-up degree
  option at the University of Hertfordshire
- NCC Education — Computing and Cyber Security
- Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport (CILT)
- Institute of Chartered Accountants, Ghana (ICAG)
- BCS (British Computer Society) Approved Centre — professional IT training

## Education and teaching
- Certificate in Early Childhood Education
- Certificate in Holistic Early Child Care Development
- Certificate in Methods and Teaching Techniques
- Executive Certificate in School Management and Leadership

## Theology, ministry and counselling
- Certificate in Theology & Church Administration
- Certificate in Theology and Pastoral Studies
- Executive Certificate in Christian Ministry
- Certificate in Counselling and Family Therapy

## Leadership and specialised
- Certificate in Alternative Dispute Resolution
- Certificate in Leadership and Governance in Health Systems Management
