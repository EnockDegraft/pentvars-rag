# Tuition and Fees (Detailed) — Pentecost University

## Undergraduate fees
Exact undergraduate tuition amounts are not published as a single public
list — they vary by programme and level and are shown on the student
e-portal and payment portal.

Mandatory non-tuition fees each semester: **Academic User Fee**, **SRC
dues**, **Pentvars Journal fee**, **Technology User Fee**.

Payment schedule:
- **Registration:** pay at least **70% of tuition plus 100% of all other
  fees** at the start of the semester to be able to register online.
- **Balance:** the remaining tuition must be paid **before revision week**
  to be eligible to sit examinations.
- Missing the registration deadline attracts a **late registration fee**.
- Registration requires a PIN generated by the fees-payment portal.

## Postgraduate fees
- **MBA** (Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Project
  Management, Logistics & Supply Chain Management, Entrepreneurship) and
  **MCom** (Taxation, Microfinance, Applied Accounting): **US$4,000**,
  payable in **5 instalments**, over 2 years.
- **MA Pentecostal / Pentecostal & Charismatic Studies**: **US$2,000**,
  payable in **2 instalments**, over 1 year.
- **University of Salford** programmes: **£3,000** in 3 instalments (1 year).
- **Buckinghamshire New University** programme: **£3,200** in 3 instalments
  (1 year).
- Fee discounts are available to applicants from the Ghana Revenue
  Authority (GRA) and the Judicial Service applying for MCom Taxation, and
  to Pentecost University alumni and active members of The Church of
  Pentecost.
