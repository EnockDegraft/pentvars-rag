# Results, Transcripts and Grade Appeals — Pentecost University

## Checking your results
- Semester results are published on the e-portal at
  https://eportal.pentvars.edu.gh under "Results" / "Academic Record".
- Results are released after the Academic Board approves them, usually a few
  weeks after examinations end.
- Notifications go to your official student email — check it and its spam
  folder.

## Results not showing / withheld
Common reasons a result is blank, marked "IC", or withheld:
- **Outstanding fees** at the time results were processed — clear the
  balance, then contact Student Records to have the record released.
- **Incomplete assessment ("IC")** — a missing component such as coursework
  or a practical. Contact the course lecturer and the department.
- **Examination irregularity under review** — the result is held until the
  case is concluded; you will be contacted by the department.
- **Registration mismatch** — you sat a paper you were not registered for,
  or vice versa. Take your registration slip to Student Records.

## Transcript requests
- **Unofficial transcript / statement of results**: available to download
  from the e-portal for your own use.
- **Official transcript** (sealed, stamped, for employers or other
  universities):
  1. Apply through the e-portal "Transcript Request" section or at the
     Student Records Office.
  2. Pay the transcript fee shown on the request (per copy).
  3. State the delivery method — collected in person, posted, or sent
     directly to an institution's address or email.
  4. Processing takes about five to ten working days; allow longer for
     postal delivery abroad.
- Transcripts are not released while you have an outstanding fee balance or
  an unreturned library book.

## Certificate collection
- Graduands collect their certificate from the Registry after
  congregation, on presentation of a valid ID and a clearance slip showing
  no outstanding fees or library items.
- A replacement for a lost certificate requires a sworn affidavit, a police
  report, a newspaper publication of the loss, and a replacement fee.

## Grade appeal / remarking
If you believe a grade is wrong:
1. First ask the course lecturer for a review — often it is an unrecorded
   coursework mark or an addition error, fixed quickly at this stage.
2. If unresolved, submit a formal remarking request to the Head of
   Department within the deadline stated when results are released
   (commonly two to three weeks). Pay the remarking fee.
3. The script is remarked by another examiner. The revised mark stands
   whether it is higher, the same, or lower.
4. A further appeal on process grounds goes to the Faculty Board and then
   the Academic Board, whose decision is final.
