# Half Scholarship (50% Tuition) — Pentecost University

The Half Scholarship covers 50% of a student's tuition fees for the
academic year. The remaining 50% of tuition, together with all non-tuition
fees (academic user fee, SRC dues, Pentvars Journal fee, Technology User
fee), stays payable under the normal payment schedule — at least 70% of the
student's outstanding balance before online registration, and the rest
before revision week.

## Who is eligible
- Admitted or continuing student with proof of enrolment.
- Demonstrated financial need, supported by documentation.
- Academic standard:
  - New students: WASSCE aggregate between 6 and 24.
  - Continuing students: cumulative GPA of at least 3.0.
- Evidence of creative or innovative skills, or a strong commitment to
  ethical leadership and service.

The Half Scholarship is the usual outcome when an applicant shows genuine
need and solid — but not outstanding — academic results, or when the
scholarship budget for the year cannot support a full award for every
qualified applicant.

## Required documents
Same as for the Full Scholarship:
- Admission letter or latest transcript.
- Two testimonials.
- Motivation letter (maximum 250 words).
- Financial documentation (pay slips, pension letters, employer letter, or
  a death certificate where applicable).
- Ghana Card or passport and two passport photographs.

## How to apply
1. Sign in to the e-portal at https://eportal.pentvars.edu.gh and open the
   "Financial Aid / Scholarship" section.
2. Complete the form and upload the supporting documents.
3. Submit before the deadline. Applications are processed March to July for
   the following academic year.

## After the award
- You are notified by email.
- The award covers one academic year and must be renewed. Renewal requires
  a GPA of at least 3.0 and good standing.
- A student may apply to be considered for a Full Scholarship at renewal if
  their circumstances or results have improved.
