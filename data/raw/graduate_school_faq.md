# Pentecost University Graduate School (PUGS) — Frequently Asked Questions

## Applying
**How do I apply?** Buy an application form for **GH¢120** (non-refundable)
on campus, or download it from the website. Submit it with 2 passport-size
colour photographs, 2 reference letters, an original academic transcript
and a certified copy of your degree certificate. International applicants
pay **US$120** and also provide a passport copy, evidence of English
proficiency, and a National Accreditation Board (NAB) degree-equivalency
letter. Post to: The Academic Registrar, P.O. Box CT 8938, Cantonments,
Accra-Ghana.

**Who is eligible?** A first degree or a chartered professional
qualification with working experience. For UK-affiliated programmes you
must show English proficiency — minimum D (SSSCE), C6 (WASSCE) or 6 (GCE
O-Level) in English, or IELTS 5.5.

**Is there a deadline?** Applications are accepted year-round for
provisional admission; full admission takes about **two months**.

**Can I apply for more than one programme?** No — one graduate programme at
a time.

## Fees
- MBA and MCom programmes (affiliated with UG/UCC): **US$4,000**, in **5
  instalments**, over 2 years.
- MA Pentecostal Studies: **US$2,000**, in **2 instalments**, over 1 year.
- University of Salford programmes: **£3,000**, in 3 instalments, 1 year.
- Buckinghamshire New University programme: **£3,200**, in 3 instalments,
  1 year.

## Registration and coursework
Pay fees at the bank, take the receipt to the Accounts Manager for a PIN,
then register online with the PIN. Attendance counts for **30%** of the
Interim Assessment, so absences lower your grade. Submit assignments to the
class representative and/or the lecturer.

## Examinations and results
Results are published online after Academic Board approval. If you are ill
during examinations, get a medical excuse from a recognised government
facility or the Pentecost Hospital, Madina, and give it to the Academic
Registry for a supplementary examination. If a grade seems wrong, discuss
it with the lecturer; if still unsatisfied, submit a Grade Complaint form
to the Academic Registrar.

**Re-sits:** a grade of **D or lower must be re-sat** (a **C** does not).
Re-sits are usually in the final trimester of the second year; register
with the Re-sit Registration form and pay the cost-recovery fee. Students
performing poorly are invited for a discussion with the Dean and/or the
Academic Registrar.

## Graduation
Pass all courses, including Research Seminars I and II, and successfully
defend your Long Essay. Pay the graduation fee once the final graduands
list is published. If you finish your programme but need more time, one
extension of up to one academic year (two semesters / three trimesters)
may be granted — no further extension.

## Other policies
- Transcripts and attestations: a small fee; introductory letters for
  research data collection are free.
- **No change-of-name policy.**
- Change of programme: allowed before registration, using the Change of
  Programme form from the Academic Registry.
- Change of session: allowed **once**, with the Dean's approval, using the
  Change of Session form.
