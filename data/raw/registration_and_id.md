# Course Registration and Student ID Card — Pentecost University

## Online course registration
1. Pay at least 70% of tuition plus all other required fees so the e-portal
   opens registration for you.
2. Sign in at https://eportal.pentvars.edu.gh and open "Course
   Registration".
3. The system loads the courses for your programme and level. Add any
   electives, check the total credit hours are within the allowed range for
   the semester, and submit.
4. Print or save the registration slip. You are not officially registered
   for a course that is not on this slip, and you may not sit its
   examination.

## Common registration problems
- **Registration is closed / blocked**: an outstanding balance, an
  unreconciled payment, or the registration window has ended. Check your
  fee balance first; if it is clear, contact the Finance Office about
  reconciliation, then Student Records to reopen the window.
- **A required course is missing from the list**: a curriculum mapping
  issue — report it to your department with your programme and level.
- **Wrong level or programme showing**: contact Student Records with your
  admission letter or last results.
- **Registered for the wrong course / wrong elective**: use add-and-drop
  during the published add-drop period (usually the first two weeks of the
  semester). After that, changes need departmental approval and may not be
  possible.
- **Late registration**: allowed for a short period after the deadline with
  a late fee; after that period you may have to defer the semester.

## Add and drop
- Open for a limited period at the start of each semester.
- Done on the e-portal under "Course Registration" → "Add/Drop".
- Dropping a course after the deadline can be recorded as a fail; get
  written departmental approval first.

## Deferment / withdrawal
- Apply in writing to the Registrar before the semester deadline, stating
  the reason (medical, financial, personal). Attach evidence.
- An approved deferment protects your place; leaving without approval is
  recorded as withdrawal and may affect any refund.

## Student ID card
- New students are issued an ID card after registration and biometric
  capture during orientation.
- **Lost or stolen card**: report it, obtain a police report for a stolen
  card, then apply for a replacement at the Student Records / ID Office and
  pay the replacement fee. A new card is normally ready within a few
  working days.
- **Damaged card or wrong details**: return the old card with a correction
  request; a reissue for a university error is free.
- The ID card is needed to enter examinations, borrow from the library,
  and collect documents from the Registry — replace it promptly.
