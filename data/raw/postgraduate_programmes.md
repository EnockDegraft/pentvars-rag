# Postgraduate Programmes — Pentecost University Graduate School (PUGS)

Postgraduate study is run through the Pentecost University Graduate School
(PUGS). Programmes are offered by weekend and evening sessions as well as
full-time, depending on the programme.

## Doctoral (PhD)
- PhD Engineering
- PhD Theology and Mission
- PhD Leadership and Corporate Governance
- (Projected) PhD Business Administration
- (Projected) PhD Healthcare Management

## Master of Philosophy (MPhil)
- MPhil Theology
- MPhil Taxation

## Master's degrees
- MSc Intelligent / Intelligent Systems Engineering
- MSc Health Planning, Policy and Management
- MA Theology
- MA Church Leadership & Administration
- MA Pentecostal & Charismatic Studies (also referred to as MA Pentecostal
  Studies)
- MCom Taxation (also Microfinance, Applied Accounting streams)
- MBA in: Finance; Marketing; Human Resource Management; Project Management;
  Logistics and Supply Chain Management; Entrepreneurship
- (Projected) MSc Herbal Medicine

Some Master's programmes are delivered in affiliation with UK partner
universities (University of Salford, Buckinghamshire New University).

## Admission requirements (MPhil / PhD)
Applicants qualify through one of several routes, including:
- an Upper Second Class first degree plus 5 years' post-qualification
  experience; or
- a Master's degree with a dissertation plus 2 years' experience; or
- a lower classification with 8+ years of significant relevant experience;
  or a registrable medical/health qualification; or a postgraduate taught
  degree; or a professional qualification obtained by written examination.
- A qualifying examination and an English proficiency test may be required.

## Admission requirements (MSc / MA / MBA)
- A Bachelor's degree, minimum Second Class Lower, **or** an HND, typically
  with a GPA around 2.5–3.0 on a 4.0 scale.
- Two reference letters, a statement of purpose and a CV.
- An entrance examination and/or interview may be required.
- MBA applicants: 2–3 years' work experience preferred; GMAT/GRE may be
  requested.
- For UK-affiliated programmes, English proficiency evidence is required —
  minimum D (SSSCE), C6 (WASSCE) or 6 (GCE O-Level) in English, or IELTS 5.5.

## How to apply
1. Buy an application form for **GH¢120** (non-refundable) on campus, or
   download it from the website. International applicants pay **US$120**.
2. Submit the form with 2 passport photographs, 2 reference letters, an
   original academic transcript and a certified copy of the degree
   certificate. International applicants also submit a passport copy,
   evidence of English proficiency and a National Accreditation Board (NAB)
   degree-equivalency letter.
3. Post to: The Academic Registrar, P.O. Box CT 8938, Cantonments,
   Accra-Ghana.
4. Applications are accepted year-round for provisional admission; full
   admission takes about **two months**. You may apply for only one graduate
   programme at a time.

## Registration
Pay fees at the bank, take the receipt to the Accounts Manager to obtain a
PIN, then register online with the PIN.

## Progression, examinations and graduation
- Class attendance counts toward the Interim Assessment (30% of the grade).
- Results are published online after Academic Board approval.
- Illness during examinations: obtain a medical excuse from a recognised
  government facility or the Pentecost Hospital, Madina, and submit it to the
  Academic Registry for a supplementary examination.
- Grade dispute: discuss with the lecturer first; if unresolved, submit a
  Grade Complaint form to the Academic Registrar.
- Re-sits: a grade of D or lower must be re-sat (a C does not); re-sits are
  usually held in the final trimester of the second year, on payment of a
  cost-recovery fee.
- Graduation requires passing all courses, including Research Seminars I and
  II, and a successful defence of the Long Essay/dissertation. A graduation
  fee is paid after the final graduands list is released.
- One extension of up to one academic year may be granted to complete;
  there is no further extension.

## Policies
- No change-of-name policy.
- You may change programme before registration (Change of Programme form).
- You may change session once, with the Dean's approval (Change of Session
  form).
