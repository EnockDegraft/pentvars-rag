# Accommodation, Library and the Academic Calendar — Pentecost University

## Student accommodation
Pentecost University has limited on-campus hall space, supplemented by
approved hostels near the Sowutuom campus. Demand exceeds on-campus supply,
so rooms are allocated in order of application and payment.

- **Applying**: open the "Accommodation" section of the e-portal at
  https://eportal.pentvars.edu.gh once it opens for the semester, choose a
  hall or room type, and pay the accommodation fee to confirm the place.
  Accommodation fees are separate from tuition and are not covered by the
  Full or Half Scholarship.
- **Priority**: first-year students, students with a disability or medical
  need, and those living far from campus are given priority. Attach medical
  evidence where relevant.
- **No room available**: ask the Hall/Accommodation Office for the list of
  approved off-campus hostels. Only use approved hostels — the university
  will not intervene in disputes with unapproved landlords.
- **Refunds**: an accommodation refund after moving out is pro-rated for
  the portion of the semester used and follows the Finance Office refund
  process.
- **Maintenance and welfare issues in hall** (plumbing, electricity,
  safety, roommate conflict): report to the Hall Warden or Senior
  Resident; unresolved matters go to the Dean of Students.

## Library services
- **Borrowing**: use your student ID card. Undergraduates may borrow a set
  number of books for two weeks, renewable if no one else has reserved the
  title.
- **Overdue books**: a daily fine accrues per book. Results and transcripts
  are withheld while you have unreturned books or unpaid library fines.
- **Lost book**: pay the replacement cost plus a processing charge.
- **E-resources and databases**: log in with your e-portal credentials from
  the library page; off-campus access uses the same login.
- **Study spaces and past questions**: past examination papers and reserved
  course texts are available at the reference desk for use in the library.
- **Clearance**: final-year students need a library clearance slip (no
  books out, no fines) before collecting their certificate.

## Academic calendar
- The academic year runs in two semesters, each with a teaching period,
  revision week, and an examination period, followed by a break.
- Key dates each semester: registration opens, registration deadline,
  add/drop deadline, revision week, examinations start, semester ends.
- The exact dates are published each year on the e-portal and on the
  noticeboards — always check the current calendar rather than assuming
  last year's dates.
- Resit / referral examinations for failed papers are held at a scheduled
  point before the next semester; you register and pay for each resit on
  the e-portal.
- Provisional results are released a few weeks after examinations; they are
  confirmed once the Academic Board approves them.
