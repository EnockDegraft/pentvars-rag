# Church Member Discount (10% Tuition) — Pentecost University

Members of The Church of Pentecost receive a 10% discount on tuition fees.
It is a standing concession, not a need-based award, so there is no
competition for it and no committee interview — a member only has to prove
membership and claim it before the fees for the semester are finalised.

## Who is eligible
- Any admitted or continuing student who is a baptised, active member of
  The Church of Pentecost.
- The discount applies to tuition only. Non-tuition charges (academic user
  fee, SRC dues, Pentvars Journal fee, Technology User fee) are paid in
  full.
- The discount cannot be combined with a Full Scholarship (which already
  covers 100% of tuition). It can sit alongside a bursary that is applied
  to non-tuition charges.

## Proof of membership
- An introductory letter from the student's local assembly, signed by the
  Presiding Elder or the District Pastor, confirming active membership.
- The letter should show the assembly and district and give a contact
  number for verification.
- Continuing students who claimed the discount in a previous year may be
  asked to provide a fresh letter each academic year.

## How to claim
1. Obtain the membership letter from your local assembly before the start
   of the semester.
2. Submit it to the Finance Office (Cash Office) in person, or upload it in
   the "Financial Aid / Scholarship" section of the e-portal, before you
   generate your invoice for the semester.
3. The Finance Office applies the 10% reduction to the tuition line on your
   invoice. Check your e-portal statement to confirm it shows before you
   pay.
4. If you have already paid full tuition before claiming, the 10% is held
   as a credit against your next semester's fees rather than refunded in
   cash.

## Common problems
- **Discount not showing on the invoice**: you claimed after the invoice
  was generated — take the letter to the Cash Office and ask them to
  reissue the invoice.
- **Letter rejected**: it was not signed by an authorised officer, or the
  assembly could not be reached for verification — get a new letter with a
  working contact number.
