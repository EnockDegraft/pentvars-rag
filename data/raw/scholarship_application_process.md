# Scholarship Application Process, Deadlines and Renewal — Pentecost University

This page covers the process that is common to the Full Scholarship, the
Half Scholarship and the Student Bursary. The Church Member Discount is
claimed differently — see the Church Member Discount page.

## Timeline for the coming academic year
- **March**: scholarship application window opens on the e-portal.
- **March to July**: applications are received and reviewed; shortlisted
  applicants are interviewed by the Scholarship Committee.
- **Late July / August**: award decisions are emailed to applicants before
  the academic year begins.
- **Bursaries** run on a rolling basis in addition to this window, reviewed
  once per semester and again before examinations.

A student who misses the window can still submit a bursary request during
the semester if a genuine hardship arises.

## Step by step
1. **Check you qualify.** Confirm the academic threshold for the award you
   want (see the individual scholarship pages) and that you can document
   financial need.
2. **Gather documents** before you start the form:
   - Admission letter (new) or latest transcript (continuing).
   - Two testimonials.
   - Motivation letter — maximum 250 words for a scholarship, up to 400
     words for a bursary hardship statement.
   - Financial evidence: pay slips, pension letters, employer letter, or a
     death certificate where a parent or guardian is deceased.
   - Ghana Card or passport and two passport photographs.
3. **Apply on the e-portal.** Sign in at https://eportal.pentvars.edu.gh,
   open "Financial Aid / Scholarship", complete the form and upload the
   documents. Combine attachments into one PDF where you can.
4. **Submit before the deadline** shown on the form. Late applications are
   not carried to the committee.
5. **Interview.** Shortlisted applicants meet the Scholarship Committee for
   a short interview — bring the originals of your uploaded documents.
6. **Decision.** You are notified by email. If awarded, you accept the
   offer on the e-portal and the reduction appears on your fee invoice.

## Renewal
- Full and Half Scholarships are granted one academic year at a time.
- To renew, reapply at the start of the next application window and show
  that you have met the GPA condition for your award (3.5 for a Full
  Scholarship, 3.0 for a Half Scholarship) and stayed in good disciplinary
  standing.
- Bursaries do not renew — reapply if the hardship continues.

## Reasons applications are refused
- Missing or unreadable documents, or a motivation letter over the word
  limit.
- Academic results below the threshold for the award.
- Financial need not evidenced, or evidence that does not match the claim.
- Submitted after the deadline.
- An unresolved disciplinary or examination-malpractice case.

## If your application is unsuccessful
- You can ask the Finance Office for brief feedback.
- You may reapply the following year, or submit a bursary request during
  the year if your circumstances change.
