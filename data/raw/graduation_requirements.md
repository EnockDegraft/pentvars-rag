# Graduation Requirements — Pentecost University

## Graduate School (Master's / PhD)
To be eligible for the award of a graduate degree, a candidate must:
- Have been properly admitted and have followed the approved course of study
  for the full programme.
- Have fulfilled all university, faculty and departmental requirements.
- Have a **Final Grade Point Average (FGPA) of 2.50 or better** at the end of
  coursework — falling short of this means the programme is deemed failed
  even if every individual course was passed (see grading system notes).
- Pass all required coursework.
- For thesis/dissertation programmes: have their dissertation or thesis
  passed by the examiners (two examiners for a Master's, three for a PhD)
  and successfully defend it at an oral examination. A plagiarised
  dissertation or thesis is rejected outright and the candidate is deemed
  to have failed.

Once these are met, results go before the Academic Board for approval, and
the degree is then conferred under the seal of the affiliate university at a
congregation. Candidates who miss a congregation because they completed
requirements late become eligible for the next one instead.

Graduates submit the corrected, final version of their thesis for
publication at least six weeks before the congregation they intend to
attend.

## Undergraduate (Bachelor's)
Confirmed points from the university's own pages:
- **Degree classification is based solely on courses taken at Pentecost
  University** — coursework transferred in from elsewhere (e.g. transfer
  students, who must complete at least six semesters at Pentecost) does not
  count toward the classification.
- The basic principle is passing all required courses; a course failed
  outright (grade F, including an F substituted for confirmed exam
  malpractice) must be retaken by re-registering and attending it again —
  there's no standalone re-sit sitting for it.

What this file does **not** yet cover with confidence: the exact CGPA
threshold, total credit-hour count, and specific degree-classification bands
(First Class, Second Class Upper/Lower, Third Class, Pass) for current
undergraduate programmes. The university's detailed 2019 Student Handbook
addresses this by pointing to its former affiliate universities' systems
(University of Ghana, KNUST, UCC), but that was written before Pentecost's
2020 Presidential Charter, so it may no longer describe the current,
independent system accurately.
