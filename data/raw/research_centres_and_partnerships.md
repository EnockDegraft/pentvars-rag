# Research, Centres and Partnerships — Pentecost University

Pentecost University describes itself as a "research-led hub for innovation
and problem-solving." Research and postgraduate work run through the
**Pentecost University Graduate School (PUGS)**.

## Journals and student research
- **PentVars Business Journal** — the university's peer-reviewed journal
  (journals.pentvars.edu.gh).
- Repositories of **students' research and project work** and **staff
  research and publications** are published on the university website.

## Centres and units
- **Pentecost Centre for the Study of Unreached People Groups (PCUPGs),
  Africa** — mission research.
- **Centre for Strategic Philanthropy & Social Investment (CESPSI)**.
- **Capacity Building, Corporate Engagement & Innovation Unit (CCI-Unit)**.
- **Chartered Institute of Professional Studies (CIPS)** collaboration.
- **International Digital Centre (IDC)** — runs an engineering programme
  (pentvarsidc.com).
- **The Global Formation Project (GPS)**.
- **Church of Pentecost Chairman's Education Foundation (COPCEF)** —
  supports grants and scholarships.

## Academic and international partnerships
- Historic affiliations (pre-charter) with **KNUST**, the **University of
  Cape Coast** and the **University of Ghana**, which mentored the
  university's early programmes.
- **Saginaw Valley State University** (USA).
- **London South Bank University**, **Buckinghamshire New University**,
  **University of Salford**, **University of Hertfordshire** and **NCC
  Education** (UK) — several postgraduate and top-up degree routes are
  delivered with these partners.

## Recent developments
- Professor Barry Mann appointed Dean of the Faculty of Health and Allied
  Sciences (2026).
- Innovation Hub partnership with the Alex Nerda Centre (2026).
- Partnership with Alveus Development to train electricians and engineers
  in sustainable transport and renewable energy (2025).
- A major **Engineering Village** construction project.
