# Detailed Entry Requirements by Route — Pentecost University

## General undergraduate entry (Level 100)
Accepted qualifications:
- **WASSCE** with credit passes in 6 subjects (3 core + 3 relevant
  electives); acceptable grades A1–C6. Minimum aggregate **36 or better**.
- **SSSCE/GBCE** with credit passes in 3 core and 3 elective subjects;
  acceptable grades A–D (grades 1–6). Minimum aggregate **24 or better**.
- **GCE 'O' Level**: 5 credit passes including English and Mathematics.
- **GCE 'A' Level**: 3 A-Level passes (minimum grade D) plus 5 'O' Level
  passes (grade 6) including English and Mathematics, and a Science or Arts
  subject. (Older WAEC GCE qualifications are no longer accepted — use the
  Mature route.)
- Post-Secondary Teachers' Certificate 'A'; approved professional
  qualifications; or success in the Mature Entrance Examination.

Core subjects: English Language; Core Mathematics; Integrated Science (for
science programmes) or Social Studies (for non-science programmes). Three
credit passes in relevant electives are also required.

Grades that do **not** qualify: SSSCE/GBCE grades E–F; WASSCE grades
D7, E8, F9.

## BSc Nursing
- 3 core subjects: English, Mathematics, Integrated Science.
- 3 electives from an approved list, e.g. General Science (Biology,
  Chemistry, Physics, Elective Mathematics), General Arts (Economics,
  Government, Religious Studies, History, French, Geography, Literature),
  Home Economics, or Agricultural Science.
- **Advanced entry to Level 300:** State Registered Nurses with 5+ years'
  experience (interview required); RGN Diploma holders with 2+ years'
  experience and a Nursing and Midwifery Council licensure pass (interview
  required).

## BSc Construction Technology / BSc Quantity Surveying
- Core: English, Core Mathematics, Integrated Science.
- Electives must include Elective Mathematics plus 2 of: Chemistry,
  Physics, Geography, Economics, Technical Drawing, Building Construction.
- Mature option: 3+ years' work experience, age 25+, 5 GCE 'O' Level passes
  (English, Mathematics, Science), and a pass in the Mature Entrance
  Examination (minimum 50% in each of English and General Paper) — entry at
  Level 100.
- CTC II/III holders with 5+ years' senior-level experience may enter at
  Level 200.

## HND and Diploma holders (entry at Level 200)
- HND classified Second Class Lower or better, plus WASSCE with 6 passes
  (A1–D7) including English and Mathematics and 3 relevant elective credit
  passes, plus 2+ years' post-HND work experience.
- Advanced Business Certificate (ABCE): a full Diploma plus 5 credit passes
  (English, Mathematics, Science/Social Studies).
- Other GTEC-accredited diplomas are considered. A Diploma in Business
  Studies (DBS) is **not** accepted for direct entry.
- HND Building Technology (weekend, entry at Level 300): First or Upper
  Second Class HND plus 2+ years' relevant experience and a one-semester
  bridging course.

## Mature applicants (Level 100)
Age 25+ with a birth certificate at least a year old, and 2+ years' work
experience. Must pass the Mature Entrance Examination and an interview —
see the separate Mature Applicants page.

## Transfer students
A minimum of six (6) semesters of full-time study must be completed at
Pentecost University; the degree classification is based only on Pentecost
University coursework.

## International applicants (Level 100)
Accepted qualifications: International Baccalaureate (IB); IGCSE / Cambridge
'O' and 'A' Levels; American High School Grade 12–13; NECO and other
national certificates verified as equivalent by Ghana's National
Accreditation Board. Typical grade expectation: credits (A1–C6 / A–D) or
IGCSE/IB grades A*/A–C in English, Mathematics and Biology (for health
programmes), with higher-level coursework where relevant. A university
interview and an English proficiency test are required; a 6-month English
Proficiency course is available. Also required: valid passport, police
clearance from the home country, Ghana Police Service registration and
regularised immigration status.
