# Accommodation and Campus Amenities — Pentecost University

## Halls of residence
Three on-campus halls: **Yeboah Hall**, **Safo Hall** and **Arnan Hall**.
Each has fully furnished JCR (Junior Common Room) lobbies for recreation.
Living on campus puts students close to classes, the library, Wi-Fi and
other facilities, and the halls are run to build "community development,
interpersonal and intrapersonal competence." The halls are supervised by
the Office of the Dean of Student Affairs.

## Off-campus housing
The **Estates and Development Office** helps students find housing in the
areas around campus and keeps information on off-campus accommodation
offered by people affiliated with the university. Students can pick a
nearby hostel or apartment for a short commute, or somewhere further out.

## Dining
A **200-seat cafeteria** serves hot meals at affordable prices, and there
are other eateries nearby.

## Getting to campus
The main campus is at **Sowutuom**, in the western part of Accra
(Ghana Post GPS: **GC-003-9632**; postal address P.O. Box KN 1739,
Kaneshie, Accra). A **shuttle bus service** and taxis run to campus from
**Lapaz** and **Kwashieman**.
