# The Library and Learning Commons — Pentecost University

## Location and hours
The **Library and Learning Commons** is on the **6th Floor of the Phase II
Building, Sowutuom Campus**.

Opening hours during the semester:
- Weekdays: **9:00 a.m. – 9:30 p.m.**
- Saturdays: **8:30 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.**

During vacations:
- Weekdays: **9:00 a.m. – 4:30 p.m.**
- Saturdays: **9:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.**

The **Learning Commons** study area is open 24 hours a day.

## Collections
A growing hybrid collection of books, journals, articles, magazines and
newspapers, in both electronic and physical formats.

## Catalogue (Destiny / OPAC)
- On campus: http://172.24.0.10
- Off campus: library.pentvars.edu.gh, or the OPAC link at
  http://ghlibrary.online:1006/
- Sign in with your university credentials. Use the **CATALOG** tab to
  search books and e-books (by keyword, title, author, subject or series —
  keyword is best if you are unsure), and **My Info** for your account.
- To reserve an item that is out or that you cannot collect immediately,
  click **Hold**. You then have about **one day** to collect it at the
  front desk before the hold expires.

## E-resources and e-library
An E-Library platform is available at http://34.122.211.24/, with
electronic databases and resources accessible using your e-portal login,
on and off campus.

## Note on clearance
Graduating students need library clearance (no books out, no unpaid
fines); results and transcripts can be withheld while library items are
outstanding.
