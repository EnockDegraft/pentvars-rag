# Student Support Offices — Pentecost University

## Office of the Dean of Student Affairs (ODSA)
Handles students' academic, residential and social welfare and helps
students "explore, identify, examine and develop their potential."
Services include:
- Letters of introduction for industrial attachment and for opening bank
  accounts
- One-to-one counselling
- Resolving disputes between students
- Support to the Students' Representative Council (SRC)
- Supervision of the halls of residence (Safo, Yeboah, Arnan)

The office oversees three units: **Mentorship**, **Student Support** (work
attachments) and the **International Students Office**.

## Counselling Unit
Located on the **2nd Floor, Phase Two Block**. Offers confidential
one-on-one counselling to students (and to staff and their dependants) on
a wide range of issues, including: academic and career counselling,
anxiety, depression, bereavement, anger management, low self-esteem,
identity problems, learning difficulties, relationships, marriage and
family, gender issues, sexual abuse and violence, phobias, panic attacks,
addiction, health issues, and workplace issues.

- The unit keeps the **highest level of confidentiality**. With the
  client's permission, difficult cases are referred to an appropriate
  specialist.

## Chaplaincy
Meets the spiritual needs of the whole university community, regardless of
religious affiliation. **University Chaplain:** Rev. Patrick Lebene Adonoo.
Programmes include Commencement and Thanksgiving Services, College Assembly,
Prayerfest, missions activities, staff devotions, and each semester a
**Relationship Summit**, an **Entrepreneurial Forum** and **Life Emphasis**
sessions with prominent guests. The Chaplaincy works with the Counselling
Unit on academic, social, psychological and spiritual support, and the
Chaplaincy Board coordinates the Christian groups on campus.

## Mentorship Programme
Aims at students' personal, relational, intellectual, professional and
spiritual development.
- **Levels 100 and 400** are mentored by faculty and staff.
- **Level 200** is paired with peer mentors from Level 400; **Level 300**
  with peer mentors from Level 300–400.
- Meeting times: Levels 100 & 300 — Tuesdays 1:15–2:15 p.m.; Levels 200 &
  400 — Thursdays 1:15–2:15 p.m.; Weekend students — Fridays 6:00–6:30 p.m.
  Mentors and mentees may also meet at other mutually convenient times.
