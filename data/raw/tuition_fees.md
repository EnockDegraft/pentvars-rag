# Tuition Fees and Payment — Pentecost University

Pentecost University describes its tuition and other academic-related fees as comparatively affordable. Exact fee amounts vary by programme and level and are published on the student e-portal and payment portal rather than as a single public list.

## Payment Schedule
- **Initial payment**: Students must pay at least 70% of tuition, plus all other required fees (academic user fee, SRC dues, Pentvars Journal fee, Technology User fee), at the start of the semester in order to register online.
- **Final payment**: Any remaining balance must be paid before revision week to remain eligible to sit for examinations.
- **Late registration**: Students who miss the registration deadline incur additional late charges.

## Scholarships and Financial Support
Pentecost University offers several forms of financial assistance to reduce the cost of tuition:

1. **Full Scholarship** — covers 100% of tuition fees.
2. **Half Scholarship** — covers 50% of tuition fees.
3. **Student Bursaries** — monetary awards determined annually by a scholarship committee.
4. **Church Member Discount** — a 10% discount on tuition fees for all members of The Church of Pentecost.

### Scholarship Eligibility
- Applicants must be admitted students with proof of enrolment.
- Must demonstrate financial need combined with strong academic performance.
- New students: WASSCE aggregate of 6–18.
- Continuing students: minimum CGPA of 3.0.
- Must show evidence of creative/innovative skills or exceptional commitment to ethical leadership.

### Scholarship Application Requirements
- Admission letter and academic transcripts
- Testimonials from teachers, counsellors, or mentors
- A letter explaining the need for the scholarship (maximum 250 words)
- Financial documentation (pay slips, pension letters, etc.)
- Identity documents and recent photographs
- Death certificate, where applicable, for financial-hardship cases

Scholarship applications are typically processed between March and July, ahead of the start of the academic year.
