# Full Scholarship (100% Tuition) — Pentecost University

The Full Scholarship covers 100% of a student's tuition fees for the
academic year. It does not automatically cover non-tuition charges such as
the academic user fee, SRC dues, the Pentvars Journal fee, the Technology
User fee, or accommodation — those remain payable unless a separate bursary
is granted.

## Who is eligible
- Must be an admitted or continuing student with proof of enrolment.
- Must demonstrate clear financial need supported by documentation.
- Academic standard:
  - New students: WASSCE aggregate between 6 and 18.
  - Continuing students: cumulative GPA of at least 3.5.
- Must show evidence of creative or innovative ability, or an exceptional
  record of ethical leadership and community service.
- Applicants with a history of examination malpractice or unresolved
  disciplinary sanctions are not considered.

## Required documents
- Admission letter (new students) or latest academic transcript
  (continuing students).
- Two testimonials from teachers, counsellors, mentors, or church leaders.
- A motivation letter of not more than 250 words explaining the need for
  the award.
- Financial documentation: pay slips, pension statements, an employer
  letter, or — where a parent or guardian is deceased — a death certificate.
- Ghana Card or passport and two recent passport photographs.

## How to apply
1. Sign in to the student e-portal at https://eportal.pentvars.edu.gh and
   open the "Financial Aid / Scholarship" section.
2. Complete the scholarship application form and upload every required
   document as a single PDF where possible.
3. Submit before the published deadline. Applications for the coming
   academic year are received and processed between March and July.
4. Shortlisted applicants are invited for a brief interview with the
   Scholarship Committee.

## After the award
- Decisions are communicated by email to the address on the e-portal.
- A Full Scholarship is granted for one academic year at a time and must be
  renewed each year. Renewal requires maintaining a GPA of at least 3.5 and
  good disciplinary standing.
- The award can be withdrawn for academic failure, proven misconduct, or if
  the financial information provided is found to be false.
