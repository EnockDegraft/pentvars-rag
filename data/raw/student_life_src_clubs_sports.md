# Student Life — SRC, Clubs, Sports and Events — Pentecost University

## Students' Representative Council (SRC)
The SRC is the "highest and official mouthpiece of students" and the
liaison between students, the administration and the wider Pentecost
University community. It works through committees — Welfare, Judicial,
Editorial and Disciplinary — that report to the Executive Council and the
General Assembly. The **General Assembly** (presidents and heads of clubs
and associations, plus faculty representatives) is the SRC's highest
decision-making body. **SRC dues** are one of the mandatory semester fees.

## Sports
On-campus facilities for tennis, basketball, volleyball and other sports.
Students compete in track and field, football, basketball and volleyball.
The university takes part in the **Private University Students Association
of Ghana (PUSAG) games** against around 19 private institutions, and has
been a strong performer (13 trophies in one edition, including golden cups
in soccer, basketball and women's volleyball, plus athletics and overall
institution awards).

## Entertainment and campus events
Organised largely by the SRC: **Akwaaba Night** (welcoming fresh students),
Sports Awards and Dinner Night, the **Fontomfrom Excellence Awards**, SRC
Week, Hall Week celebrations, Comedy Night, the Gospel reality show
**Voice of PUC**, and the **Miss Intellect PUC** and **Face of PUC**
competitions.
