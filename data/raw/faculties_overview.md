# Faculties and Schools — Pentecost University

## Faculty of Engineering, Science and Computing (FESAC)
Departments: Engineering; Built Environment; Information Technology.
Programmes: the five BEng engineering degrees (Electrical & Electronic,
Environmental, Manufacturing, Robotics & Automation, Systems), BSc
Construction Technology and Engineering Management, BSc Quantity Surveying
and Building Economics, BSc Information Technology, BSc Industrial Software
Engineering, BSc Actuarial Science, and a Pre-Tertiary Engineering
Programme. Learning is hands-on, with laboratory work, field trips,
industrial and educational tours, and industrial attachment. Graduates
progress to MSc/MPhil/PhD study or set up their own ventures.

## Faculty of Business Administration (FBA)
Departments: Accounting and Finance; Economics, Marketing and Services;
Management. Programmes include Bachelor of Commerce (Accounting with
Computing), BSc Business Administration with options in Accounting, Banking
& Finance, Marketing, and Logistics & Supply Chain Management, BSc Human
Resource Management, BA Communication Studies, BA Philosophy, Politics and
Economics, and BSc Agribusiness. The faculty takes an interdisciplinary
approach and prepares graduates for private- and public-sector careers.

## Faculty of Health and Allied Sciences (FHAS)
Departments: Nursing and Midwifery; Physician Assistantship. Programmes:
BSc Nursing, BSc Midwifery, BSc Physician Assistantship Studies (Medical),
with Doctor of Herbal Medicine and BSc Health Information Management also
associated with the faculty, and an MSc in Health Planning, Policy and
Management. Teaching is interactive with lectures, tutorials, practical
sessions, ward work and field visits. The faculty has stated an intention
to expand its health programmes and, in time, establish a medical school.

## School of Theology, Mission & Leadership (STML)
Departments: Theology; Mission; Leadership; plus a Centre for Ministerial
Formation. Programmes: BA Theology (options in Theology, Mission Studies,
Biblical Studies), Diploma in Theology & Pastoral Studies, and graduate
programmes MA/MPhil/PhD in theology, mission, church leadership and
Pentecostal studies. STML serves ministerial training within The Church of
Pentecost as well as general students.

## Faculty of Law
Programmes: Bachelor of Laws (LLB) and a Pre-Bar Course. The LLB is a
qualifying law degree for progression to the Ghana School of Law.

## Faculty of Education
