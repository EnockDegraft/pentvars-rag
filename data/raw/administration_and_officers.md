# Administration and Key Officers — Pentecost University

The central administration handles human resources, financial activities,
information technology, academic quality, public communications, internal
audit and student affairs.

## Governance bodies
- **Pentecost University Council** — the governing body of the university,
  chaired by Elizabeth Ankumah.
- **Academic Board** — the senior academic decision-making body; it approves
  examination results before they are released.
- **Chancellor:** Apostle Eric Nyamekye, Chairman of The Church of Pentecost.

## Church of Pentecost link
The university maintains a formal relationship with **The Church of
Pentecost**, and the **Church of Pentecost Chairman's Education Foundation
(COPCEF)** is a stakeholder that supports the university (including
scholarships and grants).
