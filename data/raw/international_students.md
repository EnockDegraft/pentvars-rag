# International Students — Pentecost University

The **Students Experience, Diversity and International Relations (SEDIR)
Office** (also called the International Students' Office / International
Relations Office) supports international students through application,
arrival, immigration and campus life. Contact: **iso@pentvars.edu.gh**.

## Applying
The application steps are the same as for local students — register for a
login PIN on the online application portal, complete the forms, upload
certified certificates, result slips and a passport photo, and submit.
Bring original transcripts and certificates when you arrive on campus.
You may start the process up to a year before your programme begins;
admission letters are emailed to successful applicants within one month.

## Entry requirements
Accepted qualifications include the International Baccalaureate (IB),
IGCSE / Cambridge 'O' and 'A' Levels, American High School Grade 12–13, and
other national certificates verified as equivalent by Ghana's National
Accreditation Board. Typical expectation: credit-level passes (or IGCSE/IB
grades A*/A–C) in English, Mathematics and, for health programmes, Biology.
A university interview and an English proficiency test are required; a
6-month English Proficiency course is available for applicants who need it.

## Immigration and permits
The SEDIR Office coordinates residence permits, which are renewed annually.
- **Passport:** every international student must hold a valid passport.
- **Visa:** students from ECOWAS countries do not need a visa (ECOWAS free
  movement protocol). Students from non-ECOWAS countries must obtain a visa
  before travelling.
- **Non-Citizen Ghana Card (NCGC):** application fee **US$120**, annual
  renewal **US$60**; you must appear in person at the National
  Identification Authority with your passport.
- **Residence permit documents:** admission letter copy, passport bio-data
  page, a recent fee receipt, NCGC copy, a passport photo, and your
  passport.
- **Residence permit fee:** **GH¢150** (ECOWAS) or **GH¢200** (non-ECOWAS).
- **Late-renewal penalty:** **GH¢60** (ECOWAS) or **GH¢80** (non-ECOWAS).
- Also required: police clearance from your home country and registration
  with the Ghana Police Service.

## Living on campus
International students use the same accommodation options as other students
— the three on-campus halls (Yeboah, Safo, Arnan) or off-campus housing
arranged through the Estates and Development Office — and take part in the
full range of extra-curricular programmes, forums and athletics. There is
an **International Students Association** among the registered student
clubs.
